# French glossary — for review by a francophone school

These are the French terms the app uses, or will use, for the words that
matter most in a school. They were chosen by the developers, not by a school,
so every row needs a yes or a correction from someone who runs a francophone
or bilingual establishment in Cameroon before the rest of the app is
translated. Change a term here first, then in `lang/fr.json`.

Status: **draft, not yet reviewed.**

## Open questions for the reviewer

1. **Level names.** Should a francophone school see its own level names
   (6e, 5e, 4e, 3e, 2nde, 1ère, Tle) regardless of the language chosen, since
   they are the school's data and not app text?
2. **Bilingual schools.** A bilingual school has both sections. Is one
   language per person enough, or do printed documents need both languages on
   the same page?
3. **Tone.** The app says "vous" throughout. Is that right for teachers too?
4. **Report cards and receipts.** Should they print in the school's language
   whatever the reader's own choice is?
